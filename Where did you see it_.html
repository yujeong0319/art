the study

Three AI models. Three ways to explain. One new score.

original

calculated live on this sketch

Same drawing, three ways of pointing.

Each method highlights the parts it thinks matter. We compare that with the actual pen marks. More overlap means a better explanation. We call this the CFAS score.

Drawing marks D

Every pen stroke and coloured spot. Blank paper is left out.

Grad-CAM++ –

Paints a big, soft glow. Easy to see, but it covers a lot of blank paper.

Integrated Gradients –

Marks single pixels, so it lands right on the pen strokes.

LIME –

Cuts the picture into pieces and picks the top 5. Steady, but blocky.

CFAS = overlap ÷ total area
